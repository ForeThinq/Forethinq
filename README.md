## What I build

Not generic "AI services" — each build is a working system with validation, dedupe, alerts, logging and a handover your team can actually use.

| Service | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Lead-flow automation** | Forms, inboxes & ad leads → dedupe → AI classify & score → Sheets/CRM → instant alert → follow-up |
| **WhatsApp AI assistants** | Answers from your own prices & policies, qualifies, books, logs every chat to Sheets/CRM, human handoff when needed |
| **AI data extraction** | Invoices, forms, PDFs & messy CSVs → clean, validated rows — duplicates removed; scraping & list cleanup with AI enrichment |
| **Workflow fixes & rescues** | Broken n8n / Make automations diagnosed, rebuilt & monitored with failure alerts |

## How I work

1. **Audit** — we map where leads or data are leaking today.
2. **Scope** — you approve a fixed price before any build starts.
3. **Build** — the system is built inside your own accounts and tools.
4. **Handover** — you get alerts, documentation, a walkthrough and fix support.

- ✅ Fixed price agreed before work starts — a small paid test is welcome
- ✅ Built inside **your** accounts (your n8n/Make, your Sheets/CRM, your WhatsApp number)
- ✅ Every workflow ships with error alerts, a README, and a walkthrough video
- ✅ Payments via Wise / UPI
